## Design Guidelines

### Color Scheme
- **Primary**: Dark blue (#1a237e) - Professional, institutional
- **Secondary**: Gold/Amber (#ffb300) - Premium, financial
- **Accent**: Green (#4caf50) - Growth, success
- **Background**: Dark (#0a0e27) or Light (#ffffff) - Professional

### Typography
- **Headings**: Bold, sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter, or similar)
- **Body**: Clean, readable (Open Sans, Roboto, or similar)
- **Numbers**: Large, bold for emphasis
- **Consistency**: Same font family throughout

### Visual Style
- **Clean & Professional**: Institutional, not flashy
- **Data-Driven**: Charts, graphs, metrics prominently displayed
- **Minimal Text**: One message per slide
- **High-Quality Icons**: Consistent icon set (Feather, Material, or custom)
- **Professional Images**: High-res, relevant visuals

### Slide Transitions
- **Simple**: Fade or slide (not flashy)
- **Consistent**: Same transition throughout
- **Fast**: Quick transitions, don't linger

- **Why**: AI-powered formatting, professional templates, auto-design

- **Why**: Easy to use, many templates, good for non-designers
- **Template**: Search "pitch deck" or "startup presentation"

- **Why**: Full design control, professional quality, custom everything

- **Features**: Advanced design tools, collaboration, prototyping
- **Cost**: Free tier available, paid for teams

## Presentation Tips

### Delivery
1. **Practice**: Know your deck inside and out
2. **Timing**: 10-15 minutes presentation, 10-15 minutes Q&A
3. **Energy**: Confident, passionate, but professional
4. **Pause**: Let key points sink in
5. **Eye Contact**: Engage with investors, not just slides

### Key Messages to Emphasize
1. **"Only L1 with native order matching"** - Repeat this
2. **"$18.5T market opportunity"** - Massive TAM
3. **"Standard Chartered, XDC Network partnerships"** - Proven connections
4. **"Sub-second finality, provable fairness"** - Technical superiority
5. **"Regulatory compliance by design"** - Institutional ready

### Common Questions to Prepare For
- "Why now?" → Regulatory tailwinds, institutional adoption
- "What's your moat?" → Native matching + network effects
- "Who are your competitors?" → No direct competitor with same tech
- "What's your go-to-market?" → Standard Chartered pilot → partnerships
- "What if Ethereum adds this?" → Hard to replicate (consensus-level changes)
