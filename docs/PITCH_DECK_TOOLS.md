## 📊 Slide-by-Slide Checklist

Use this checklist when creating your deck:

- [ ] Slide 1: Cover (logo, tagline, professional)
- [ ] Slide 2: Problem ($18.5T gap, pain points)
- [ ] Slide 3: Solution (native matching, key features)
- [ ] Slide 4: Why Now (regulatory, adoption, timing)
- [ ] Slide 5: Market ($18.5T TAM, breakdown)
- [ ] Slide 6: Technology (architecture, competitive)
- [ ] Slide 7: Use Cases (RWA + Credit, examples)
- [ ] Slide 8: Traction (partnerships, pilots)
- [ ] Slide 9: Competitive (comparison table)
- [ ] Slide 10: Team (advisors, connections)
- [ ] Slide 11: Business Model (revenue, projections)
- [ ] Slide 12: Roadmap (Q1-Q4 milestones)
- [ ] Slide 13: Financials (growth, metrics)
- [ ] Slide 14: The Ask ($10-20M, use of funds)
- [ ] Slide 15: Contact (thank you, QR code)

## 🚀 Pro Tips

1. **Keep It Simple**: One message per slide
2. **Use Data**: Numbers > adjectives
3. **Visual Hierarchy**: Important info = larger, bolder
4. **Consistency**: Same fonts, colors, style throughout
5. **Practice**: Know your deck, practice delivery
6. **Get Feedback**: Share with advisors before presenting
7. **Version Control**: Keep different versions for different audiences
8. **Export Options**: Have PDF, PowerPoint, and web versions ready
